Privacy Notice

This notice explains what FXGuard AI collects when you create an account, why it is needed, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Your verified phone number or email address for signing in and account recovery.
  • The payment checks you choose to save, including currency, invoice amount, check period, risk result, likelihood probability, costs, BNR rate date and model version.
  • Your feedback ratings and comments, plus your name, import category or phone number only when you choose to provide them.
  • Limited security records needed to protect accounts and investigate misuse.

FXGuard AI does not need bank details, card numbers, supplier contact details or copies of invoices. Please do not enter them.

Why we use the information

  • To create and secure your account.
  • To save your checks and make them available when you return on another device.
  • To provide downloads, deletion and other account controls.
  • To maintain security and meet applicable legal obligations.

Your phone number or email is not used for marketing unless you separately choose to receive marketing messages.

Service providers and storage

Account authentication and database storage are provided through Supabase. Phone codes require a configured SMS provider, and email codes use the configured email provider. The application is hosted through Render. These providers may process or store information outside Rwanda. Required registration, contracts and external-storage authorisation must be completed before accounts are enabled for real users.

How long we keep information

Saved checks remain while your account is active so you can return to them. You may delete one check, clear your history or delete your account. Information is removed when it is no longer needed, subject to limited security, backup and legal requirements.

Your choices and rights

You can access and download your account information, delete saved checks and delete your account. To request a correction or make another privacy request, use the Share feedback page and write Privacy request at the start of your message.

Device-only checks

Guest checks stay in your browser. They are uploaded to your account only after you choose to save them. Clearing browser data may remove device-only checks.

Feedback

The feedback form is provided within FXGuard AI. Validated responses are stored in a local Excel backup and forwarded to the project's Google Forms response sheet. Feedback is handled separately from your FXGuard AI account and is not linked to your account unless you clearly choose to identify yourself.

This is a plain-language product notice. It should be reviewed by a Rwanda-qualified privacy professional before production use.

Terms of Use

These terms explain how FXGuard AI may be used and what its payment checks do and do not mean.

FXGuard AI estimates RWF depreciation risk using recent and past BNR exchange-rate information. It supports business planning. It does not provide financial advice, promise a future exchange rate, transfer money or decide when a supplier should be paid.

You remain responsible for payment and business decisions. Consider supplier terms, available cash, professional support and current market information alongside this platform.

Account suspension or closure

Changes to these terms

If an important change affects account use or personal information, users should be shown the new version and asked to accept it before continuing.

What the risk levels mean

We compare recent exchange rates with past BNR rates. The result is Low, Medium, or High risk.

Low

The recent rate has stayed within its usual range.

  • The estimate shows a smaller chance of added cost during the selected period.
  • Recent movements have been limited compared with the model's past data.
  • The rate can still change before payment.
Medium

Recent changes show that this payment may cost more.

  • The estimate shows a moderate chance of added cost during the selected period.
  • The displayed extra cost is a planning scenario, not a guaranteed future cost.
  • Payment timing, available cash and supplier terms remain business factors.
High

Recent changes show that this payment could cost much more.

  • The estimate shows a higher chance of added cost during the selected period.
  • The displayed extra cost shows the possible impact under the planning scenario.
  • The result does not decide when, how or whether to make the payment.

Use these risk levels for planning only. We cannot promise what the exchange rate will do next.
