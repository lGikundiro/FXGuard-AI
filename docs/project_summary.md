# FXGuard AI Project Summary

FXGuard AI is a full-stack exchange-rate risk classification and payment-planning system for Rwanda-based importers. It classifies 7-day and 14-day depreciation pressure for USD/RWF, EUR/RWF, and KES/RWF as Low, Medium, or High using engineered features from official National Bank of Rwanda (BNR) exchange-rate histories.

The current prototype includes:

1. Multi-currency payment checks for USD, EUR, and KES
2. Six production classifiers, covering every currency and 7-day/14-day horizon
3. Risk probabilities, recent-rate drivers, RWF cost estimates, and payment considerations
4. Dashboard rate history and data-freshness reporting
5. Excel result exports
6. Optional one-time-code accounts with user-owned cloud history
7. Check deletion, account data export, account deletion, consent, privacy, and terms flows
8. Guest device history and participant feedback collection
9. A FastAPI backend and responsive JavaScript frontend deployed separately on Render

The bundled histories come exclusively from the official BNR Excel exports in `data/raw/`; private bank, supplier, or business records are not used for model training. Model selection compares logistic regression, random forest, and XGBoost with forward-only rolling-origin backtesting, horizon-specific purge gaps, and a final untouched chronological holdout.

The current production data and models are generated by `scripts/sync_multicurrency_rates.py` and `scripts/train_multicurrency_models.py`. Notebooks `01` through `05` preserve the earlier USD/RWF teaching workflow and are not the source of the active multicurrency artifacts.
